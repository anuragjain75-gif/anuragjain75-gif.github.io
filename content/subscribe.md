Some essays take months to write.

Some begin as quiet observations during an evening walk, while others grow silently over years before they find their final form.

Lilamaya has no publishing schedule and no algorithm to satisfy. New writing appears only when it feels complete.

If these essays have become a place you enjoy returning to, you are warmly invited to subscribe.

Subscribers receive an email whenever a new essay or fragment is ready.

Nothing more.

No newsletters.

No advertising.

No promotions.

Only new writing.
