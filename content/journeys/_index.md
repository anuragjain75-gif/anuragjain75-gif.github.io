---
title: "Journeys"
description: "Explorations through literature, philosophy, art and the landscapes of the mind."
---

Some subjects invite more than a passing encounter. A book leads to another book; a writer opens a door into an unfamiliar world; a question follows us through literature, philosophy and the ordinary circumstances of life.

Journeys preserve something of these explorations: the discoveries, associations, questions and unexpected paths through which an intellectual landscape gradually takes shape.
